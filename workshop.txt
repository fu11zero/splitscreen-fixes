title=Splitscreen Fixes (Split Screen / Local Co-op)
description=[h1]Splitscreen Fixes[/h1]
description=Fixes for split screen (splitscreen, local co-op, couch co-op: 2-4 players with gamepads on one PC) in Build 42: hanging item transfers, cars, gun aiming, world map, crafting, building, vehicle mechanics, moodles and fliers. Works when hosting from the main menu, on a dedicated server and, for the UI fixes, in offline splitscreen. Needs ZombieBuddy.
description=
description=[h2]Fixed[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Stuck item transfers.[/b] When two splitscreen players move items at the same time (loot window, smoking takes the lighter out of a bag, equipping, building), one action fills the progress bar to 100% and hangs without a result. The players on one PC share a network connection, and the server parsed player 2's request over player 1's pending one. Now every splitscreen player gets their own request, the same as a player on another PC.
description=[*][b]Car that doesn't move for the server.[/b] A splitscreen player drives, but the car stays parked for everyone else, the road is blocked at the chunk edge, and on exit the car snaps back. The game only sent the car position when the driver was the "current" local player. Now any local player holding the car sends it.
description=[*][b]Firearm aiming.[/b] Only the first player's crosshair closed in on zombies; for the others it stayed wide open and targets weren't highlighted. Now aiming works for every splitscreen player.
description=[*][b]World map per player.[/b] The map was one for everybody, covered the whole window, stopped drawing the world on every screen and froze all players while one of them read it. Now each player has their own map on their own screen, and only that player stands still. A gamepad no longer gets stuck on a map another player closed.
description=[*][b]Crafting and building per player.[/b] Opening a crafting window closed everyone else's, and the build button could build through another player's window. Now each player has their own windows, opened on their own screen.
description=[*][b]Vehicle mechanics window[/b] opens on its player's screen instead of the middle of the window, and shrinks to fit.
description=[*][b]Fliers and brochures per player.[/b] A flier one player read closed at once while another player had the map open, and "show on map" opened the map for the wrong player. Now each player's flier window works only for them and stays on their screen.
description=[*][b]Clock and temperature per player.[/b] There was one clock for everybody in the middle of the window, showing the temperature of the first player with a watch, and its alarm button changed everyone's alarm. Now every player with a watch has their own clock in the corner of their screen, with the temperature where they are.
description=[*][b]Search mode (foraging).[/b] For every player except the first, search mode darkened the whole screen with no visible area around the character. Now every player sees their search circle.
description=[*][b]View cone.[/b] Players other than the first had no field of view cone (the area behind the character was not darkened). Now every player has their own view cone.
description=[*][b]Cheats (debug mode, admin).[/b] Invisible, God mode, No clip and other cheats from the Cheats panel applied only to one player. Now they apply to every local player, and a player who joins later gets them too.
description=[*][b]Context menus.[/b] A long right-click menu (a pot full of recipes) was limited only by the whole window and covered the player below. Now menus and submenus stay on their player's screen and scroll when they don't fit.
description=[*][b]Confirmation dialogs.[/b] Dialogs like "forget the whole map", a request from another player to inspect you, invites, kill animal or remove zone opened in the middle of the whole window, often on someone else's screen, and some could only be answered with the mouse. Now they open in the middle of their player's screen and take that player's gamepad focus.
description=[*][b]Moodles.[/b] With 3-4 players the upper players' moodle column ran onto the lower players' screens. Now the icons shrink to fit the player's screen.
description=[/list]
description=The map, crafting, mechanics, moodle, flier and aiming fixes are client-side and work in offline splitscreen too.
description=
description=[h2]Options[/h2]
description=Settings - Mods - Splitscreen Fixes. The layout changes can be turned off one by one if you prefer the vanilla way (for example a shared map on a small monitor): own world map, flier window, own crafting windows, mechanics window, context menus, dialogs on the player's screen, own clock. Bug fixes are always on.
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] 2.3.4+ installed as a Java agent (see its install guide).
description=[*]Enable Splitscreen Fixes in the main menu Mods list [b]and[/b] in the host server settings (Host - Manage settings - Mods).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Host from the main menu[/h2]
description=The game starts the host server as a separate process without ZombieBuddy. This mod adds ZombieBuddy to that process. The server loads Java mods you approved with "remember" or whose author you trust; for any other Java mod ZombieBuddy shows its dialog while the host starts (denied after 30 seconds).
description=
description=[h2]Dedicated server[/h2]
description=Start the server with ZombieBuddy (-javaagent:ZombieBuddy.jar) and add the mod to the server. Only the item transfer fix needs the mod on the server; the rest is client-side and works on any server.

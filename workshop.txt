title=Splitscreen Fixes (Split Screen / Local Co-op)
description=[h1]Splitscreen Fixes[/h1]
description=Fixes for split screen (splitscreen, local co-op, couch co-op: 2-4 players with gamepads on one PC) in Build 42: hanging item transfers, cars, gun aiming, world map, crafting, building, vehicle mechanics, moodles and fliers. Works when hosting from the main menu, on a dedicated server and, for the UI fixes, in offline splitscreen. Needs ZombieBuddy.
description=
description=[h2]Fixed[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Stuck item transfers.[/b] When two splitscreen players move items at the same time (loot window, smoking takes the lighter out of a bag, equipping, building), one action fills the progress bar and hangs. Players on one PC share a connection, and the server mixed up their requests. Now each player's request is handled on its own.
description=[*][b]Car that doesn't move for the server.[/b] A splitscreen player drives, but for everyone else the car stays parked, the road is blocked at the chunk edge, and on exit the car snaps back. Now any local driver sends the car position.
description=[*][b]Firearm aiming.[/b] Only the first player's crosshair closed in and highlighted targets. Now aiming works for every player.
description=[*][b]World map per player.[/b] The map was one for everybody, covered the whole window and froze all players. Now each player has their own map on their own screen, and only that player stands still, even when bumped.
description=[*][b]Crafting and building per player.[/b] Opening a crafting window closed everyone else's, and the build button could build through another player's window. Now each player has their own windows, opened on their own screen.
description=[*][b]Vehicle mechanics window[/b] opens on its player's screen instead of the middle of the window, and shrinks to fit.
description=[*][b]Fliers and brochures per player.[/b] A flier closed at once while another player had the map open, and "show on map" opened the wrong player's map. Now each flier works only for its player, on their screen.
description=[*][b]Clock and temperature per player.[/b] One clock in the middle of the window showed the first player's temperature and alarm. Now every player with a watch has their own clock in the corner of their screen.
description=[*][b]Search mode (foraging).[/b] For every player except the first, search mode darkened the whole screen with no visible area around the character. Now every player sees their search circle.
description=[*][b]View cone.[/b] Players other than the first had no field of view cone (behind the character was not darkened). Now everyone has one.
description=[*][b]See-through trees.[/b] In a forest, the see-through circle around every player but the first was torn, then gone. Now it works for everyone.
description=[*][b]Cheats (debug mode, admin).[/b] Cheats from the Cheats panel applied only to one player. Now to every local player, including ones who join later.
description=[*][b]Context menus.[/b] A long right-click menu (a pot full of recipes) was limited only by the whole window and covered the player below. Now menus and submenus stay on their player's screen and scroll when they don't fit.
description=[*][b]Other windows.[/b] Inspect clothing / sewing and other windows opened across the seam onto another player's screen. Now they are moved onto their player's screen.
description=[*][b]Confirmation dialogs.[/b] Dialogs like "forget the whole map", inspect requests or invites opened in the middle of the whole window, some answerable only with the mouse. Now they open on their player's screen with that player's gamepad focus.
description=[*][b]Saved characters in any order.[/b] On a server, a saved character chosen out of order got another character's skills, a broken inventory and lost progress; "Add new player" could overwrite a saved one. Now both work, and a refused join can be retried. Needs the mod on the server.
description=[*][b]Moodles.[/b] With 3-4 players the upper players' moodle column ran onto the lower players' screens. Now the icons shrink to fit the player's screen.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Added[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]One player per monitor[/b] (off by default). With 2+ players the borderless window covers all monitors, each player gets their own. On tiling WMs (Hyprland, sway) the window must be floating.
description=[/list]
description=The map, crafting, mechanics, moodle, flier and aiming fixes are client-side and work in offline splitscreen too.
description=
description=[h2]Options[/h2]
description=Settings - Mods - Splitscreen Fixes. Each layout change (own map, flier, crafting, mechanics, context menus, windows and dialogs, clock) can be turned off if you prefer vanilla. One player per monitor is off by default. Bug fixes are always on.
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] 2.3.4+ installed as a Java agent (see its install guide).
description=[*]Enable Splitscreen Fixes in the main menu Mods list [b]and[/b] in the host server settings (Host - Manage settings - Mods).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Host from the main menu[/h2]
description=The game starts the host server without ZombieBuddy; this mod adds it. Java mods you approved with "remember" or by a trusted author load; for others ZombieBuddy shows its dialog (denied after 30 s).
description=
description=[h2]Dedicated server[/h2]
description=Start the server with ZombieBuddy (-javaagent:ZombieBuddy.jar) and add the mod to the server. The item transfer and saved character fixes need the mod on the server; the rest is client-side and works on any server.
description=
description=Source code and technical details: [url=https://github.com/fu11zero/splitscreen-fixes]GitHub[/url] (README.md).
